Refund, Dispute & Settlement Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026 · Transparent per-minute billing with guaranteed rapid resolutions for dropped calls or unsatisfactory sessions.

1. 60-Second Instant Drop Guarantee

We understand network fluctuations can occur. If an audio or video call disconnects within the first 60 seconds of connection, our automated billing engine cancels the session with zero deduction. 100% of your reserved wallet funds are automatically unlocked immediately.

2. Eligibility for Consultation Refunds

A complete or partial refund credit to your Devotee Wallet will be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Severe Audio/Video Disturbance: Agora RTC network jitter or packet loss exceeding 40% rendering the conversation unintelligible.
  • Astrologer Inactivity: The astrologer failed to respond, remained silent, or was unresponsive during the active consultation timer.
  • Incomplete Paid Report: Personalised PDF reports not delivered within the promised 24–48 hour delivery timeline.
  • Duplicate Recharge: Unintentional multiple payments debited through UPI or payment gateway for a single recharge request.

3. Online Puja Booking Cancellations

Online Puja rituals involve temple logistics, offering preparations, and assigning Vedic priests:

  • Before Priest Assignment: Full 100% refund credited back to your original payment method or Devotee Wallet.

  • Post-Puja Ceremony: Once the ritual is solemnized with video proof, refunds cannot be processed.

4. Astro Store Gemstone & Product Returns

All physical merchandise (energized gemstones, Rudrakshas, Yantras) are backed by our 7-Day Transit Replacement Guarantee. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or with an unsealed laboratory certification certificate, notify support@namhastro.com within 7 days of delivery for a free courier exchange or full refund.

5. Dispute Filing & Resolution SLA

To claim a refund, click "Report Issue" on your consultation log in /account or email support@namhastro.com with your consultation ID. Our operations team reviews the server logs and resolves all verified claims within 2 to 4 business hours.
